Throughout my internship at Amazon Web Services Vietnam Company Limited (participating in the hands-on Workforce Bootcamp - First Cloud AI Journey) from October 01, 2026 to January 01, 2027, I had an invaluable opportunity to hone my technical thinking, expand practical engineering competencies, and apply foundational academic knowledge to an international-standard enterprise cloud environment.

Over the course of the 12-week internship, I successfully:

  • Completed the comprehensive 12-week hands-on training roadmap (Worklog), mastering core AWS services (IAM, VPC, EC2, S3, RDS, DynamoDB, Lambda, ECS Fargate, CloudFront, CloudWatch).
